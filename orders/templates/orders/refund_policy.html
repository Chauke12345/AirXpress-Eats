Refund and Cancellation Policy

We want customers to have a positive experience with AirXpress Eats. This policy explains how to report an order problem and request a cancellation or refund.

Please note: Refund requests are assessed according to the circumstances of the order and applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy removes rights that customers have under applicable South African law.

1. Cancelling an order

If you need to cancel an order, contact the relevant shop or AirXpress Eats support as soon as possible. Cancellation may be more difficult once a shop has started preparing the order or a driver has collected it. We will assess the request based on the order's status and the circumstances.

2. Incorrect, missing or damaged items

If an order arrives with an incorrect or missing item, or an item is damaged, contact support promptly. Please provide your order details and a clear description of the problem. Photographs may help us investigate where appropriate.

3. Late or undelivered orders

Delivery times can be affected by traffic, weather, shop preparation and other circumstances. If your order has not arrived or there is a significant delivery problem, contact support so the order can be checked and an appropriate resolution considered.

4. Online payments and order confirmation

AirXpress Eats uses a one-time online checkout payment for individual orders. Once payment has been successfully verified, the order is intended to be confirmed automatically.

If your payment appears successful but you do not receive an order confirmation, contact AirXpress Eats support and provide your order details and payment confirmation. We will investigate the transaction and order status.

If you believe you have been charged more than once for the same order, contact support with the relevant payment references so we can investigate and arrange an appropriate correction or refund where applicable.

5. Refund assessment

Each request is assessed individually. Depending on the circumstances, possible resolutions may include a correction, replacement, cancellation or refund where appropriate. All decisions must comply with applicable South African consumer law.

6. Refund processing

Where a refund is approved or otherwise required, it will generally be returned through the original payment method where possible. Processing times depend on the payment provider and the customer's bank. We will communicate any relevant processing information available to us.

7. How to request help

Contact AirXpress Eats support as soon as possible and provide your order number, the contact details used for the order, and a description of the issue. Keep relevant receipts or supporting evidence until the matter is resolved.

8. Consumer rights

This policy must be applied consistently with applicable South African law, including any consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

9. Policy updates

AirXpress Eats may update this policy as its services and procedures develop. Customers should review the current version when using the platform.
