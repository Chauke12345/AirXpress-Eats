Refund and Cancellation Policy

We want customers to have a positive experience with AirXpress Eats. This policy explains how to report an order problem and request a cancellation or refund.

Please note: Refund requests are reviewed according to the circumstances of the order and applicable consumer law. Nothing in this policy removes rights that customers have under applicable South African law.

1. Cancelling an order

If you need to cancel an order, contact the relevant shop or AirXpress Eats support as soon as possible. Cancellation may be more difficult once a shop has started preparing the order or a driver has collected it. We will assess the request based on the order's status and the circumstances.

2. Incorrect, missing or damaged items

If an order arrives with an incorrect or missing item, or an item is damaged, contact support promptly. Please provide your order details and a clear description of the problem. Photographs may help us investigate where appropriate.

3. Late or undelivered orders

Delivery times can be affected by traffic, weather, shop preparation and other circumstances. If your order has not arrived or there is a significant delivery problem, contact support so the order can be checked and an appropriate resolution considered.

4. Refund assessment

Each request is assessed individually. Depending on the facts, possible resolutions may include a correction, replacement, cancellation or refund where appropriate. A refund is not guaranteed for every request, and any decision must comply with applicable consumer law.

5. Refund processing

If a refund is approved, the payment method and processing time will depend on the payment provider and transaction. Some refunds may take additional time to appear in the customer's account. We will communicate any relevant processing information available to us.

6. How to request help

Contact AirXpress Eats support as soon as possible and provide your order number, the contact details used for the order, and a description of the issue. Keep any relevant receipts or supporting evidence until the matter is resolved.
